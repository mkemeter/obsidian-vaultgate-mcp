# Apu Nahasapeemapetilon

## Role

External SRE consultant from **Kwik-E-Mart Cloud Solutions LLC**. CGHMN's primary escalation contact for on-call incidents. Available at all hours — unlike some internal engineers, he answers his pager. Every time. Including the three times [[Homer Simpson]]'s pager went unanswered and Apu had to escalate directly.

"Thank you, come again." — his standard sign-off, even on incident reports.

## Engagement

Kwik-E-Mart Cloud Solutions holds the SRE retainer for CGHMN's production infrastructure. Apu is the named point of contact. He has been on the retainer long enough to have developed strong opinions about Friday deployments.

He wrote the incident report for [[Incident - The Great Donut Outage]]. It was precise, factual, and noted — in calm professional language — that this was the third production incident caused by a Friday deployment by the same engineer. He did not name [[Homer Simpson]] in the summary. He named him in the appendix.

## Style

Apu is efficient, patient, and slightly long-suffering in the way of someone who has seen too many avoidable incidents to be surprised by one more. His reports are models of clarity. His escalation procedures are the basis for the [[On-Call Runbook]], which [[Lisa Simpson]] formalized.

He does not complain. He does document.
