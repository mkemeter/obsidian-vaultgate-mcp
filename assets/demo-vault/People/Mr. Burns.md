# Mr. Burns

*Full name: Charles Montgomery Plantagenet Schicklgruber Burns.*

## Role

CEO and primary investor of Compu-Global-Hyper-Mega-Net. Does not attend engineering meetings. Does not use email directly. Is aware, somehow, of everything. Communicates exclusively through [[Smithers]].

Mr. Burns is ancient, aristocratic, and operates with the calm certainty of someone who has survived worse than a missed SLA. "Excellent." is his most common response to good news. His response to bad news involves the phrase "release the hounds," which CGHMN does not have, though nobody has tested this assumption.

## What He Wants

- **Everything monitored.** "I want to know when a server sneezes, Smithers."
- **Savings reported in megabucks.** Any cost reduction must be quantified this way. Standard currency is acceptable as a backup but will receive a disappointed look.
- **Status reports. Frequently.** See [[Exec Review - Burns Q4]]. The "Q4" is a formality; Burns requests status reports regardless of quarter.
- No surprises. Any surprise is treated as a personal affront.

## Interacting with Burns

Do not interact with Burns directly. Route everything through [[Smithers]]. If Burns speaks to you directly, respond briefly, confirm the number in megabucks, and exit. Do not mention Fridays, donuts, or [[Homer Simpson]] unless asked.

Burns was pleased with [[Operation Donut]]'s cost savings. He said "Excellent" twice, which Smithers confirmed is a high-water mark.

Manages [[Smithers]] (VP Engineering). Investor oversight of all active projects.
