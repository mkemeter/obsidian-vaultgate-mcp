# Springfield DMV

## Company

The Springfield Department of Motor Vehicles. Responsible for driver's licensing, vehicle registration, eye tests, written examinations, and making the process of each as unpleasant as legally permitted. The DMV has operated continuously since the mid-1980s. Its core systems have operated continuously since 1987. These are not separate facts.

CGHMN was contracted by the [[City of Springfield]] to assess the DMV's legacy systems and propose a modernisation path. The DMV was not consulted before the contract was signed. This has defined the relationship.

## Contact

**Patty Bouvier** and **Selma Bouvier** — twin sisters, senior clerks, and the de facto technology decision-makers at the Springfield DMV. Both chain-smoke. Both speak in the measured monotone of people who have processed one too many driving test failures. Both regard any proposal to change the current system as a personal attack.

*"We're the Department of Motor Vehicles, not the Department of Happy Feelings."*

*"The system has worked since 1987. Why would we change something that works?"*

*"No."* — Patty, in response to every migration proposal received to date, including the one submitted with a 47-page technical justification written by [[Lisa Simpson]].
