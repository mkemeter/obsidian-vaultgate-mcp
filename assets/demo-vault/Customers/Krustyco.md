# Krustyco

## Company

Krustyco is the licensing and entertainment empire of **Krusty the Clown** (legal name Herschel Shmoikel Pinchas Yerucham Krustofsky). The portfolio includes Krusty Burger, Krusty-O's cereal, Krusty Brand home pregnancy tests, and a line of merchandise that has generated more product liability cases than revenue. Krustyco's business model is: put Krusty's face on something, cash the cheque, investigate the recall later.

The company is in a perpetual state of financial restructuring. Krusty has filed for bankruptcy at least once and owes significant back taxes. Despite this, he continues to receive substantial licensing advances, which he immediately loses at the racetrack.

## Contact

**Krusty the Clown** — CEO, primary client contact, and main source of scope change. Jaded, chaotic, and perpetually distracted. Misses approximately 40% of scheduled calls. When he does attend, requirements are delivered in the format: "Can you just make it funnier? I don't know, make the buttons bigger. Also more clown."

*"They drive a dump truck full of money up to your house and you just… you can't say no. I'm not made of stone!"* — Krusty, explaining his contract with Krustyco (about Krustyco).

## Engagement

**Project:** [[Operation Big Top]]
**Status:** `active` — health `yellow`

Replatforming krusty.com. The site currently runs on infrastructure so old it predates the Krusty brand refresh of the mid-90s. [[Milhouse Van Houten]] owns the frontend rebuild. [[Comic Book Guy]] has opinions about the backend that he has not expressed constructively.

The vague requirements ("make it more Krusty") have necessitated three rounds of discovery. [[Bart Simpson]] found a vulnerability in staging during [[Sprint S35E08]] by exploiting a session token issue, which is how he ended up on probation and how the security audit got added to the project scope.
