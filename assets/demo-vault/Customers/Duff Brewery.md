# Duff Brewery

## Company

Duff Brewery is the manufacturer of **Duff Beer** — Springfield's dominant beer brand and one of CGHMN's most demanding clients. The brewery is run by CEO **H.K. Duff**, a man of few words and strong opinions about launch parties. The Duff brand is managed with the full intensity of a company that considers beer not just a product but a way of life.

Duff Beer has many variants: Duff Lite, Duff Dry, Lady Duff, Duff Dark, Raspberry Duff, and Tartar Control Duff, among others. The telemetry infrastructure behind their brewing operations, however, is a single undocumented SQL Server from 2004. This is the problem [[Project Fermentum]] is attempting to solve.
