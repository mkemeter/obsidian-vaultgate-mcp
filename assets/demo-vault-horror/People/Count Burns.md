# Count Burns

CEO and investor. Ancient, aristocratic, and — since the acquisition of the Springfield Blood Bank — no longer strictly alive. Communicates only through [[Slithers]]. Measures everything in megabucks. Says "Excellent." when pleased and "Release the hounds!" when not.

Wants everything monitored. Knows the names of all his projects. Knows the name of [[The Stonecutters API]], which is strange, because nobody told him.

Does not know who the [[Gelatinous Homer|Senior Cloud Engineer]] is, and must not be reminded. **Do not mention Fridays, ooze, or the Senior Cloud Engineer unless asked.**

Signs his internal memos "No. 1." I have chosen not to ask about this.
