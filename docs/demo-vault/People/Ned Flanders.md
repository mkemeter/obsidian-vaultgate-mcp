# Ned Flanders

## Role

QA Lead at CGHMN. Enthusiastic. Relentlessly positive. Opens every code review with "Hi-diddly-ho, neighborino!" and means it. The feedback that follows is precise, thorough, and occasionally devastating — but always delivered with a smile and a scripture-flavored metaphor. Engineers have learned that "just one small diddly of a concern" is Ned's way of saying "this will not pass review."

## Review Style

Ned's PR comments follow a consistent three-part structure:

1. **Cheerful opening.** "Hi-diddly-ho! What a delightful piece of code!"
2. **The nit.** Specific, technically correct, and impossible to argue with.
3. **Closing metaphor.** "As Proverbs says, a house built on sand will not stand — and neither will a function with no error handling. Okely-dokely!"

He has reviewed more PRs than anyone else on the team. His approval rate is high. His standards are higher.
