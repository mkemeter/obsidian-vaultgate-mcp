# Comic Book Guy

*Real name: Jeff Albertson.*

## Role

Senior Backend Developer at CGHMN. The most technically experienced engineer on the team. Also the least approachable, least collaborative, and most likely to leave a 47-comment PR review with no actionable summary. His feedback is almost always correct. This does not make it easier to receive.

## Review Philosophy

"Worst. PR. Ever." is not hyperbole. It is a calibrated signal that something has gone wrong. The problem is that Comic Book Guy deploys this signal for both critical architectural errors and minor variable-naming conventions. Engineers have stopped being able to tell the difference.

His review process:
1. Read the entire PR.
2. Leave comments on every line he disagrees with.
3. Save the most sweeping verdict for the final comment.
4. Close the review as "Request Changes."
5. Repeat next sprint.
