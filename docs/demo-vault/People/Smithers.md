# Smithers

## Role

VP Engineering at CGHMN. Reports to [[Mr. Burns]]. Manages Quinn Hopper and, by extension, the entire engineering organization. Available at all hours — this is not a perk, it is simply how Smithers operates. He has never been observed leaving the building before Burns does.

"At once, sir." — his most frequent response to anything Burns says.

## How He Works

Smithers is competent, organized, and deeply committed to ensuring that [[Mr. Burns]] is never displeased. In practice, this means that any request from Burns arrives at Quinn's desk within minutes, worded with maximum urgency and minimum context.

He is not unkind. He is efficient. The distinction matters.

Smithers keeps meticulous records of Burns' requests, which he sometimes shares proactively when he thinks Quinn might find them useful. This is as close to solidarity as his role allows.
