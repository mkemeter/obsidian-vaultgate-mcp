# Springfield Mafia

*Operating entity: Legitimate Businessman's Social Club, LLC.*

## Company

Springfield Mafia LLC is, per their own documentation, a legitimate business operation. "Any resemblance to organized crime is purely coincidental." CGHMN provides business software services to their holding company, registered under various names. The primary trading entity for technology procurement is the **Legitimate Businessman's Social Club**.

Their software requirements are specific, professionally communicated, and occasionally difficult to fulfill within standard compliance frameworks. They pay on time. In cash.

## Contact

**Fat Tony** — full name Anthony "Fat Tony" D'Amico. Real first name, per a slip by an associate: **Marion**. Calm, soft-spoken, mock-formal. Delivers requirements with the measured precision of someone who has thought carefully about the consequences of ambiguity.

*"This is a legitimate business operation. Any resemblance to organized crime is purely coincidental."*

*"We would be most… appreciative if the audit logs were configured with appropriate selectivity. There are certain transactions that, for business confidentiality reasons, need not be… preserved."*

His emails are short, grammatically correct, and slightly menacing in a way that is very hard to formally object to.
