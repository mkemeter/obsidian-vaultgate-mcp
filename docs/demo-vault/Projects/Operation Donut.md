# Operation Donut

## Objective

Internal initiative to reduce CGHMN's cloud infrastructure costs. No external customer. Initiated after [[Mr. Burns]] asked [[Smithers]] to ask me why the cloud bill "had more zeros than a Springfield lottery."

Project codename: **Donut** — named not for the pastry (though [[Homer Simpson]]'s involvement made this inevitable) but because the cost savings review had a habit of going in circles every time Homer was in the room. And because Homer kept ordering donuts for the review sessions. And because the biggest single saving came from decommissioning an instance Homer had labelled `donut-backup-prod-FINAL-v3`.

## Status: Shipped #shipped

**Outcome:** Saved **several megabucks** over the review period. [[Mr. Burns]] was informed via [[Smithers]] and said "Excellent" twice, which Smithers confirmed was a positive sign.

Key actions taken:
- Decommissioned 14 unused instances, including `donut-backup-prod-FINAL-v3` (owner: [[Homer Simpson]], last accessed: unknown)
- Renegotiated three service contracts identified by [[Lisa Simpson]] as auto-renewing without review
- Consolidated dev/staging environments for [[Operation Big Top]] and [[Project Monorail]]
- Implemented budget alerts that notify me before they notify [[Mr. Burns]]

Reviewed in [[Exec Review - Burns Q4]]. Burns requested the savings be maintained "indefinitely." Smithers took the action. I took the action of writing it down here.
