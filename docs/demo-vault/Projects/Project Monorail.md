# Project Monorail

## Objective

Modernise the Springfield city permit portal — the online interface through which residents apply for building permits, event licences, noise exemptions, and other municipal approvals. The current portal is a static HTML form that emails a PDF to an unmonitored inbox.

Project codename: **Monorail** — a tribute to Springfield's most famous infrastructure investment. My idea. It was meant as a gentle warning.

## Status: Planning

The project is in early planning. [[Lisa Simpson]] is the lead and has already produced a risk register with four items that [[City of Springfield#Contact]] has not acknowledged.

**Original scope:** Simple permit lookup and application submission.

**Current scope (after stakeholder additions):**
- Online permit lookup and submission ✓
- Mobile-optimised interface
- Multilingual support (English, Spanish, and "whatever language the tourists speak")
- Integration with the county assessor's database (which may not have a public API)
- A "citizen satisfaction dashboard" for the Mayor's office
- A public-facing mayoral message widget that Quimby wants to update himself
- Accessibility compliance to a standard Quimby described as "the best, very best"

**Budget:** Unchanged from original scope.

## Risks

1. Scope has expanded 6x without budget adjustment. Flagged. Unacknowledged.
2. The county assessor's database integration may require a separate procurement process. Nobody at the city knows who owns it.
3. [[City of Springfield#Contact]] attends kickoffs and milestone events. Nothing between. Decision latency is measured in weeks.
4. The project may be politically motivated (mayoral election cycle). Scope and priority could change without notice.
